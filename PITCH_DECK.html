The Solution

PawShelter: Trustless Pet Adoption

PawShelter returns blockchain technology to its fundamental promise: public verifiability, tamper-proof audit trails, and elimination of middlemen.

  • 100% On-Chain State Machine: Available → Pending → Adopted
  • Direct Human Connection: Rescuer-to-adopter application flow
  • Anti-Abuse Protections: Prevents self-adoption and duplicate approvals
  • Zero Speculation: Focused entirely on verified animal rehoming

Product Experience

Simple, Modern, Delightful

1. Publish Listing

Owners input pet name, species, age, photo, and story directly to the Botchain smart contract.

2. Express Interest

Adopters submit household details and contact info on-chain without sharing sensitive data publicly.

3. Finalize Adoption

The owner approves the best fit. Once handed over, the milestone is permanently sealed on-chain.
