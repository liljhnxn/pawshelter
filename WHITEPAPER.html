1. Executive Summary

Every year, over 6.3 million companion animals enter shelters in the United States alone. Traditional adoption systems suffer from centralized gatekeeping, high administrative overhead, fragmented record-keeping, and widespread pet scams.

PawShelter provides a decentralized public registry on BOT Chain Mainnet where every listing, adoption expression of interest, owner approval, and finalized adoption is immutably recorded on-chain without intermediaries, databases, or speculative tokens.

2. Problem Statement

  • Opaque Intermediaries: Traditional pet listing platforms charge adoption fees and lock adoption history inside closed proprietary servers.
  • Adoption Fraud & Scams: Classified websites have no cryptographic verification, leading to thousands of online deposit scams and illegal pet-flipping.
  • Speculative Distraction in Web3: Previous blockchain pet projects treated animals as speculative NFTs or yield tokens rather than solving real-world welfare problems.

3. The PawShelter Solution

PawShelter introduces an on-chain state machine for pet adoption:

[Owner Lists Pet] ──> Status: Available
       │
       ▼
[Adopter Applies]  ──> AdoptionInterest Recorded On-Chain
       │
       ▼
[Owner Approves]   ──> Status: Pending (Locked to Approved Adopter)
       │
       ▼
[Milestone Done]   ──> Status: Adopted (Permanent Historical Record)

5. Security & Access Control

The contract strictly adheres to Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI). Listing owners cannot apply to adopt their own pets. Once an adopter is approved, no other applicant can be approved concurrently. Adoptions can only be finalized by either the listing owner or the verified approved applicant.

6. Conclusion

PawShelter demonstrates that blockchain ledgers can solve real-world animal welfare coordination problems through transparency and trustlessness.
